# research-decision-log

> Research decision tracking, rationale preservation, trade-off
> documentation, and methodological memory.

## 1. Purpose

This file records important research decisions.

The goal is to preserve:

-   why a choice was made;
-   alternatives considered;
-   evidence supporting the choice;
-   consequences;
-   later changes.

Research decisions should not exist only in memory.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Decisions That Should Be Logged

Log decisions involving:

-   research question changes;
-   dataset selection;
-   sampling strategy;
-   variable definition;
-   metric selection;
-   model selection;
-   baseline selection;
-   preprocessing;
-   exclusion criteria;
-   experiment protocol;
-   statistical method;
-   interpretation boundary.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Decision Quality Check

Before accepting a decision:

Ask:

1.  What problem does this decision solve?
2.  What evidence supports it?
3.  What alternatives were considered?
4.  What trade-off does it create?
5.  What risk remains?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Decision Change Protocol

When changing a previous decision:

Record:

-   previous decision;
-   new decision;
-   reason for change;
-   evidence causing the update;
-   impact on previous results.

Do not silently rewrite history.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Principles

**Document decisions, not just outcomes**

**Reasoning matters as much as the final choice**

**Changed decisions require traceability**

**Trade-offs should be visible**
